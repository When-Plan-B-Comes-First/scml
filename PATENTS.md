# Patents

The methods implemented in this repository are covered by **four U.S. patent
applications filed in 2026** by Dr. Ahmed Elmahdi, spanning structural
clustering algorithms, native high-dimensional clustering, supervised and
unsupervised evaluation frameworks, and density-aware sampling with
parameter-transfer deployment.

## Filed applications

| # | Application No. | Filing date | Title | Track |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 63/969,070 | Jan 27, 2026 | ADABOX: Adaptive Density-Based Box Clustering with Parameter Generalization | Low-D |
| 2 | 63/969,078 | Jan 27, 2026 | SCOPE: Structured Clustering Optimization via Performance Evaluation | Low-D |
| 3 | 64/056,834 | May 4, 2026 | AdaGraph: Graph-Based Adaptive Density Clustering with Scale-Invariant Parameter Transfer and Sample-Learn-Classify-Deploy Pipeline | High-D |
| 4 | 64/131,353 | Aug 11, 2026 | Graph-SCOPE: Graph-Topology-Based Clustering Validation Index with Decomposable Structural Components for High-Dimensional Unsupervised Learning | High-D |

## Coverage relative to this repository

The **Low-D track shipped here** — AdaBox, SCOPE, and the low-D SLCD parameter
transfer — is covered by applications 1 and 2. The parameter-generalization and
scale-invariance mechanisms that make sample-tuned parameters transfer to full
datasets are claimed in application 1.

The **High-D track** (AdaGraph, Graph-SCOPE, DA-Sampler), marked "coming soon"
in this repository, is covered by applications 3 and 4.
